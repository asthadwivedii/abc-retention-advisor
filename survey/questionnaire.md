# Managerial User Study — Questionnaire

**Title:** Would you trust an AI to tell you who might leave your team?
**Tool reviewed:** ABC Ltd Employee Retention Advisor (Streamlit app)
**Format:** Google Form, ~8 minutes. Respondents first use the app (or watch a 3-minute demo), then answer.
**Consent line (top of form):** *This survey is part of an academic assignment at IRMA. Responses are anonymous and used only for this study. You may skip any question.*

---

## Section B — Rate each statement (1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree)

| Construct | Code | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| Perceived usefulness | PU1 | This tool would help me spot employees at risk of leaving earlier. |
| | PU2 | The pay check (expected pay) is useful for my decisions. |
| | PU3 | The tool would improve the quality of my retention decisions. |

| | TR2 | I would rely on the tool's prediction when deciding whom to talk to. |

## Section C — Scenario

**C1.** The tool says one of your best team members has a **72% (High)** chance of leaving. From your own experience, this person seems happy and loyal. What would you most likely do?
- Follow the tool: start retention actions now
- Follow my judgement: ignore the score
- Talk to the employee first, then decide
- Look for more data (HR records, peer feedback) before acting
